    Imitating a small village, Hotel Pyin Oo Lwin is arguably the best place to stay in town, with a high level of modern amenities, warm service and good location just ten minutes from the Kandawgyi Botanical Gardens.

    The 36 teak bungalows are incredibly spacious and decorated in a very traditional, English ‘club house’ style with wood panelling, classic furnishings and lighting. Complimentary WiFi is available, you have your own private veranda, plus there is a heated swimming pool, an in-house restaurant serving Chinese cuisine, bar and free shuttle service.

    We offer a number of private and interest-led tours and activities around Pyin Oo Lwin; please speak to your travel designer about how you wish to pass your time here.

    Located in the heart of Myanmar, 67 km east of Mandalay, in the highlands of the Mandalay Mountains, the city has a cool climate and was used as a summer residence by the British until the Second World War. choice for both business and leisure travelers.

    At an altitude of 1070 meters, the Aureum Palace Hotel & Resort, Pyin Oo Lwin is not only a hotel with garden bungalows that offer all the famous Aureum comfort, but also the completely restored residence of the former British governor of Burma. Where once officers, lords, and ladies sought refuge from the summer heat, conferences, weddings, and business meetings take place today in a nostalgic colonial flair. Even the journey from the busy town of Mandalay is worth the visit. There is only one direction while on the serpents to Pyin Oo Lwin: upwards.

    Enjoy the elegance and splendor of the past and traditional luxury in a unique colonial style where you can experience the ambiance of a century gone while enjoying the modern facilities of luxurious bungalows.

    Situated 17 minutes' stroll from Sabai Sabai Foot Massage, the 3-star Hotel Shwe Nann Htike Pyin Oo Lwin features Wi-Fi throughout the property and parking on site. Conveniently located a 5-minute ride from Ruby Mart Pyin Oo Lwin, which is a great place to shop, this Pyin Oo Lwin hotel provides 27 rooms.

    The property is about 90 km from Mandalay International airport and 10 minutes by car from such cultural venues as Butterfly Museum. Bagan is 42 km from the Shwe Nann Htike Pyin Oo Lwin, while Old Colonial Building is a 5-minute stroll away. National Kandawgyi Botanical Gardens is situated 3 km from the accommodation.

    The rooms comprise a balcony and a seating area complete with ironing facilities and air conditioning, as well as high-speed internet and a flat-screen TV with satellite channels. They are appointed with a sofa and a work desk, and also feature a mini fridge bar. Bathrooms come with a bidet, a bathtub, and a roll-in shower, along with hair dryers and dressing gowns. Guests can have breakfast in the restaurant.
